Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026. This notice covers the TillThimble pilot waitlist. A fuller policy will apply when the app launches.

Who we are

TillThimble is a bookkeeping service for Kenya's micro-merchants. Contact us at tillthimble-2@polsia.app.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and type of business.
  • Anything you choose to tell us in messages.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about the pilot and invite you to try the app.
  • To learn what small businesses need so we can improve the product.

Your consent

We only use your details because you ticked the consent box and sent them. You can withdraw consent at any time by messaging or emailing us.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers we use to run this website and to message you, such as hosting, email and WhatsApp. These providers may process data on servers outside Kenya.

How long we keep it

Until the pilot ends or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond promptly. You may also contact Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

This service is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date above.
